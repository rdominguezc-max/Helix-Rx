# Project Vision

helix is an intelligent platform designed to improve the continuity of patient care by connecting patients, families and healthcare professionals through technology.

Our mission is not simply to remind people to take medication.

Our mission is to help people stay on treatment, reduce preventable complications and improve their quality of life.

---

# The Problem

Millions of patients interrupt or abandon their treatments because of:

* Forgotten medications.
* Poor treatment adherence.
* Lack of follow-up.
* Poor communication with healthcare providers.
* Fragmented medical information.

These situations often result in:

* Preventable medical crises.
* Hospital admissions.
* Increased healthcare costs.
* Reduced quality of life.

---

# Our Solution

helix provides a single platform to support patients throughout their treatment journey.

Core capabilities include:

* 💊 Medication management.
* ⏰ Smart reminders.
* 📋 Digital medical record.
* 🚨 Crisis and symptom tracking.
* 📅 Medical appointments.
* 👨‍⚕️ Physician dashboard.
* 👨‍👩‍👧 Family follow-up.
* 📈 HELIX Score.
* 🚦 Risk Indicator.
* 📊 Clinical analytics.
